Free guide

Shoot your product
right the first time.

7 simple tips with no gear. A good starting photo makes the final result better, whether you use it yourself or send it to us.

Tip 1

Shoot next to a window

Side daylight is the best free light. Turn off the flash and ceiling lights so colours stay true.

Tip 2

A plain, clean background

White card, a plain wall or a pattern-free sheet. Remove everything else from the frame.

Tip 3

Phone at product height

Shoot front-on with the phone at the product's height, not from above or below, so the shape isn't distorted.

Tip 4

Fill the frame

Move closer until the product fills most of the shot with a little space around it. Don't zoom; move your hand.

Tip 5

Wipe the lens and the product

One wipe of the lens makes a big difference to sharpness. Clean dust and fingerprints off the product.

Tip 6

Shoot every side

Front, back, side, and a close-up of the label or any key detail.

Tip 7

Send it as a file on WhatsApp

Send the photo as a Document, not a Photo, so WhatsApp doesn't compress it.
